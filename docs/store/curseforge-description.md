# Dragon Chess

**A real-time match-3 puzzle with RPG combat, playable in a window inside World of Warcraft Forever.**
Perfect for queue times, flight paths and waiting for the raid to fill.

Swap gems to match colours. Every match damages the enemy; matches of four and five create **skill** and
**ultimate** gems that cast your class's spells. Fight through ten enemies of classic Azeroth in four stages — from a Core
Hound to Nefarian — before they take you down. There are no turns: the enemy attacks on its own timer, so you
play fast and plan ahead.

## Features

- **Your class, your spells.** All nine classes have their own kit of 12 spells (two per gem colour) with
  the real names, icons, sounds and spell effects: Cleave, Whirlwind and Shield Block for Warriors, Polymorph and
  Frost Nova for Mages, Chain Lightning for Shamans, and so on. Your character's class is preselected; you can pick
  any other on the start screen.
- **Real-time combat.** Stun, freeze or delay the enemy, block and reflect its attacks, and heal yourself with a
  heal over time or a big emergency heal.
- **Enemies and bosses of classic Azeroth** with their models, sounds and mechanics (shields, curses, phases).
- **Five difficulty levels** and local best scores.
- **Pause anywhere.** Closing the window pauses the run; it also hides when you enter combat and comes back
  afterwards. A run survives `/reload`.
- Three gem looks (Jewels, WoW icons, Shapes for better readability), sound and screen-shake options.

## How to play

- `/dchess` or the minimap button opens the game.
- Drag a gem onto its neighbour (or click one, then the other) to swap.
- Match 3 to deal damage, 4 for a skill gem, 5 or more for an ultimate gem. Match a skill or ultimate gem to cast it.
- Watch the enemy's attack queue on the right and answer in time.

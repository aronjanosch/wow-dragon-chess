easier, so the duration went to 12 s (the fallback named in the spec); the +100 stays.

Open finding (not changed): a shield stack blocks one hit of any size and stacks without limit. One Emerald skill
(4-match) prevents about one mean hit (510–664 HP at D3, more at higher difficulty), more than a Rejuvenation
(500 HP, does not scale) — the strongest defensive spell is a skill. Candidate: cap the stacks (e.g. 3).

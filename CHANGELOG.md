# Changelog

## 0.3.1

- Shield spells (Power Word: Shield and its class versions) stack up to three times; a cast at the limit shows "Shield max".

## 0.3.0

- **New gem art:** hand-painted gems made for this addon replace the old set.
- **Fire Shield and its class versions** (Thorns, Retaliation, Retribution Aura, ...) last 12 s instead of 10 s and
  return every enemy hit plus 100 damage.
- **Faster enemies:** Hellhound, Harpy and Qiraji Scarab attack more often with smaller hits (same damage per
  second as before), so reflecting pays against them and single shields pay against the heavy hitters.
- Fixed: the enemy model could be far too large after `/reload`.

## 0.2.0

- **Class kits:** all nine Classic classes have their own 12 spells (two per gem colour) with their real names
  and icons, plus matching sounds and spell effects.
- **Class picker** on the start screen; your character's class is preselected. A run keeps its class.
- **Amber is the healing colour:** the skill is a heal over time, the ultimate a strong instant heal
  (replaces lifesteal and the gem conversion).
- Status icons (shield, reflect, heal, enemy stun or freeze) show your class's spell.
- Corrected spell icons (Hammer of Justice, Mind Control, Arcane Explosion, Whirlwind, Flamestrike, Rain of Fire).
- Start and pause menu, result screen and options panel in one consistent look.
- Between-fight transition: "Cleared", boss heal moment, next enemy announcement.
- Smoother effects during big chains; calmer damage streaks and ability gem auras.
- Option: hide the window when you enter combat and reopen it afterwards.

## 0.1.0

- First public version: real-time match-3 board, 12 abilities, ten Classic enemies in four stages, five
  difficulties, local best scores.
